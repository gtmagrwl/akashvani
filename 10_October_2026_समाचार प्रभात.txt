Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate projects worth 13,000 crore rupees in Chennai, Tamil Nadu today. The Prime Minister will flag off the metro rail service of the Chennai Metro Rail Project, built at a cost of around 4900 crore rupees. Mr Modi will also undertake a ride on the Chennai Metro from Porur Bypass Station to Poonamallee Bypass Station and back. The Poonamallee – Porur corridor features a unique 3.7-km double-decker structure with advanced signalling systems designed to support driverless operations. The Prime Minister  will dedicate and inaugurate railway infrastructure projects worth over 750 crore rupees including five Amrit Stations at Palani, Vellore Cantonment, Ambasamudram, Paramakudi and Namakkal, which have been redeveloped under the Amrit Bharat Station scheme. Mr Modi will perform Darshan and Pooja at the Jalakandeswarar Temple near the Porur lake. More from our correspondent:

“ The stage is all set for the Prime Minister’s visit today at Chennai. The city has brightened up to welcome the Prime Minister as he is set to launch several projects based on infrastructure in road development , ports and innovation in sciences. This includes laying  the foundation stone and inauguration of  key National Highway projects to strengthen maritime infrastructure, expand cargo-handling capacity and improve logistics efficiency. The road projects will  reduce the travel time apart from easing traffic congestion in National Highways in Salem , Madurai and Nagapattinam. He will also lay the foundation stone  for  Lube Oil Base Stocks  project at the existing Manali Refinery of Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited. Prime Minister will also inaugurate an additional Coal Berth project and lay the foundation stone for capital dredging works for additional  Container Terminal at Kamarajar Port . Prime Minister will also participate in the Golden Jubilee celebrations of Jaigopal Garodia Hindu Vidyalaya School in Chennai before leaving to Delhi. Joy For Akashvani , Chennai .”

Pradhan Mantri Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana has completed one year today.  The scheme was launched on this day last year to strengthen agriculture in 100 Aspirational Agricultural Districts across the country. Our correspondent reports that the scheme has brought together 36 Central schemes across 11 Ministries and Departments, along with State schemes and private sector participation, to address district-specific agricultural needs.

“ India’s agriculture sector has significant potential across diverse regions and farming communities. Approved by the Union Cabinet in July last year, the six-year initiative was launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 11th October last year. It brings together 36 Central schemes across 11 Ministries and Departments, along with State schemes and private sector participation, to address district-specific agricultural needs. Pradhan Mantri Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana completed its first year, today marking a year of focused district-level action. During the first year, all 100 selected districts prepared their action plans, which are under implementation. NITIKA GUPTA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI”

Navratri, the 9-day long festival to worship Goddess Durga, begins today with religious fervor and gaiety.

During Navratri, the nine forms of Goddess Durga are worshipped. On the first day, Devi Durga is worshiped as Maa Shailaputri, the daughter of the Himalayas, who represents strength, stability, and new beginnings.

In Gujarat, Navratri- the longest dance festival of the world, begins with utmost enthusiasm and religious fervour . With the beginning of the festival today, all the cities across the state have geared up for the massive Ras Garba celebrations. We have a report from our correspondent:

“ Across the state, Navratri is celebrated with utmost joy by lighting the traditional Garbo lamp, observing nine day fasts, performing rituals like ghat sthapan and by performing traditional garba dance. On the first day of the Shardiya Navratri, devotees are reaching in large numbers to the famous goddess temples in the state including the most revered Ambaji temple, Mahakali temple in Pavagadh, and Mata Na Madh in Kutch. To ensure safety during the festivities, the Gujarat Police has made elaborate security arrangements across more than 22,000 Garba venues. Special ‘She-Teams’ and large number of police personnel are deployed at party plots and street Garba grounds to ensure women’s safety. APARNA KHUNT, AKASHVANI NEWS, AHMEDABAD.

The Bathukamma festival, Telangana’s special festival of flowers, began yesterday with Engilipula Bathukamma. More from our correspondent:

“ The celebrations will continue for nine days. Women make the Bathukamma using colourful flowers like Tangedu, Gunugu, marigold and chrysanthemum. The flowers are arranged in a tall, dome-like shape. A small idol of Goddess Gauramma, made of turmeric, is placed on top. In the evening, women gather around the Bathukamma. They sing folk songs and clap their hands as they dance in circles. At the end, the Bathukamma is immersed in a pond or lake. Each of the nine days has a different name. After Engilipula Bathukamma on the first day, come Atukula, Muddapappu, Nanabiyyam, Atla, Aligina, Vepakayala and Vennamuddala Bathukamma. The festival ends on the last day with Saddula Bathukamma. A different food offering is made to the Goddess each day. This festival worships nature. It stands for the unity of women and the rich culture of Telangana. Sapthagiri, AKSHVANI NEWS , Hyderabad”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has completed 25 years in public service, a journey defined by the mantra of Jan Seva Se Desh Seva, and a relentless commitment that continues. Guided by this vision for Seva and Sushasan, the Government has been ceaselessly working towards achieving the goal of a Viksit Bharat by 2047. Through inclusive development and social empowerment, this dedication is transforming lives. Today, in our special series, we bring you an instance of how this vision has been translated into action through Gujarat’s Port Revolution.

“ Gujarat boasts the longest coastline among all Indian states, but for decades, its true maritime potential remained underutilized. Shifting the approach from viewing the coast as mere geography to treating it as vital industrial infrastructure, Narendra Modi, as Chief Minister, pioneered a dynamic model of private capital and public-private partnerships. Ports were seamlessly integrated with power grids, road networks, rail corridors, and industrial estates, creating an end-to-end supply chain. Landmark ports like Mundra, Hazira, and Pipavav flourished under this vision. This successful Gujarat model eventually laid the foundation for national maritime transformation under the Sagarmala programme, boosting India’s total port capacity from 1,561 million tonnes per annum in 2014-15 to over 2,817 million tonnes per annum, while cutting turnaround times at major ports by half. Highlighting India’s record-breaking maritime achievements and green initiatives, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said.

In Saudi Arabia, attack on the international airport in Riyadh, has killed 12 people and injured more than 300 others. The strike, which hit a domestic flight terminal complex yesterday, killed four Saudi citizens, two Bangladeshi nationals, and one citizen each from the United States, Egypt, Jordan, Syria, Sudan, and Palestine.

This incident is part of a sudden surge in attacks targeting Saudi airports, military infrastructure, and oil facilities following a recent offensive by a Saudi-led coalition against Iran-backed Houthi rebels in neighboring Yemen.

While the Houthis claimed responsibility for earlier airport strikes that killed three people, they have not yet reacted on the recent attack, though they did accuse Saudi Arabia of bombing the airport in the Yemeni capital of Sanaa.
